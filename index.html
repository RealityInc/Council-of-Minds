Make the world work for 100% of humanity
Buckminster Fuller believed the world already produces enough for everyone to flourish. The problem is not scarcity — it is design. He called this challenge the World Game: use the best available data and human intelligence to make civilization work for all life.
"The purpose of our lives is to add value to the people of this world." — Buckminster Fuller
A note on personal wellbeing
Fuller understood that the personal and the planetary are inseparable. You cannot solve the world while neglecting yourself. The Oracle exists for this reason — your real questions, your specific situation, deserve the same quality of attention as global resource flows. Personal clarity is the foundation of civilizational change.
The Tools
🌍
World Game
Fuller's civilizational simulator. Real data from the World Bank on energy, food, water, and wealth. Tap any country for its actual numbers. Move sliders to model a better future. Ask the council what it means.
🪞
Oracle
Bring your real questions. A council of thinkers from across history assembles to offer perspective — practical, philosophical, personal. Completely private. Never stored.
⬡
Forum
Convene great minds around a question that matters. Watch them disagree, challenge each other, and converge on something neither could reach alone. Share the synthesis to the Gallery.
✦
Create
Express an idea through art. The council writes in their own voices, then weaves them into one collective piece. Art is how ideas move — use it to draw people into what you care about.
⊞
Gallery
What people have made and shared. Scenarios, debates, poems, manifestos. The commons of the World Game — collective intelligence made visible.
⊕
Council
Choose who advises you. Surprise yourself with a random council from across history, name specific thinkers, or let the AI curate voices relevant to your questions. The council speaks across all tools.
The World Game is a project of The Livingry Foundation — named after Fuller's concept of livingry: tools that support life, as opposed to weaponry. All data belongs to its contributors. No data is sold. No ads. The intelligence generated here is a commons.
